Our point of view

Built around the pieces you actually reach for.

Northline is a fictional studio created to explore a more restrained approach to fashion presentation. The visual system uses strong type, generous spacing and editorial photography instead of decorative clutter.

The collection mixes formal pieces, everyday layers and accessories. The site is designed to make that range easy to browse without losing the feel of a lookbook.
